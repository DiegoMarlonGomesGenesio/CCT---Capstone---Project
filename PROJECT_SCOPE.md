# Project Scope

## Project Title
Predicting Peak Electricity Demand to Support Operational Planning

## Project Aim
To analyse historical electricity consumption patterns and develop predictive models to forecast electricity demand one hour ahead, helping identify peak-demand periods across three distribution zones in Tetouan, Morocco.

## In Scope
- Analyse the Power Consumption of Tetouan City dataset from the UCI Machine Learning Repository.
- Validate and prepare historical electricity consumption and weather data.
- Identify consumption patterns by hour, day and distribution zone.
- Investigate relationships between weather conditions and electricity demand.
- Create time-based and historical consumption features.
- Develop and compare baseline, Linear Regression and Random Forest forecasting models.
- Evaluate predictions using MAE and RMSE and assess the identification of peak-demand periods.
- Present results through charts, a final report and a simple dashboard.

## Out of Scope
- Real-time electricity monitoring or forecasting deployment.
- Direct control of electricity distribution infrastructure.
- Predicting equipment failures or power outages.
- Calculating actual financial savings or electricity costs.
- Forecasting demand for cities outside Tetouan.
- Integrating with live utility company systems.

## Project Duration
The project will be developed over two semesters.

### Semester 1
- Define the business problem and project objectives.
- Review relevant literature.
- Validate the dataset and conduct exploratory analysis.
- Plan the predictive modelling methodology.

### Semester 2
- Prepare forecasting features and datasets.
- Train and compare predictive models.
- Evaluate forecasting accuracy and peak-demand identification.
- Develop visualisations and a simple dashboard.
- Complete the final report and presentation.

## Expected Business Value
The project aims to demonstrate how short-term electricity demand forecasts can support operational planning by helping anticipate high-demand periods. Potential operational benefits will be discussed, but actual cost reductions or avoided outages cannot be measured using this dataset alone.
