## Project Overview

**Project Title:** Predicting Peak Electricity Demand to Support Operational Planning

This capstone project aims to analyse historical electricity consumption data from three distribution zones in Tetouan, Morocco.

The main business problem is the difficulty of anticipating periods of high electricity demand, which can affect operational planning.

The project will investigate electricity consumption patterns and develop predictive models to forecast short-term electricity demand and identify peak-demand periods.

### Project Objectives

1. Explore electricity consumption patterns across three distribution zones.
2. Investigate relationships between weather conditions, time and electricity demand.
3. Develop and compare predictive models for one-hour-ahead electricity demand forecasting.
4. Evaluate model performance and present findings to support operational planning.
